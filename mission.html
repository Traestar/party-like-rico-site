Mission & Vision

We exist to Realize Individuals Celebrate Occasions — connecting people with unforgettable experiences across Atlantic City and the Jersey Shore.

The Brand Meaning

Every letter carries meaning. Every event carries purpose. RICO exists to help people recognize that every occasion is worth celebrating — and we make sure they do it right.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect people in Atlantic City and the surrounding shore towns with unforgettable events, experiences, and celebrations — bringing together local businesses, visitors, and community members through the power of shared moments.

We coordinate events that deliver real energy, genuine connections, and lasting memories. Whether it's a shuttle party experience, a vendor pop-up, a community event, or a private celebration, our mission is the same: make it extraordinary.

🌟

Our Vision

To become Atlantic City's most trusted and recognized event promotion brand — a platform that locals and visitors turn to first when planning any experience in the city.

We envision a future where Party Like Rico Promotions is synonymous with premium Atlantic City experiences — expanding into event media coverage, curated travel itineraries, year-round event programming, and a thriving network of community and business partnerships.
