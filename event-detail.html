About This Event

This Isn't Just a Crawl — It's an Experience

Secure your seat for our first shuttle party event of 2026 and celebrate 420 weekend in style. Join us for the AC Bud Crawl, where attendees will enjoy a guided tour of various venues connected to the holiday vibe while riding in a party shuttle.

This is more than just a crawl — it's an experience bringing together great energy, local businesses, and unforgettable moments throughout Atlantic City. Whether you're a local or visiting for the weekend, this is the event you don't want to miss.

What's Included
  • Shuttle bus transportation to multiple venues
  • Access to exclusive 420-friendly experiences
  • Curated stops at participating businesses
  • High-energy atmosphere with like-minded attendees
🎁 Special Perk: The first 50 attendees will receive complimentary giveaways from participating businesses!
Important Information
  • Must be 21+ to attend (valid ID required)
  • Tickets are limited — secure yours early
  • Event runs rain or shine
  • Exact check-in location will be announced one week prior to the event

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring? +
Valid government-issued photo ID (must be 21+), your ticket confirmation, cash or card for purchases at venues, and good energy. Dress comfortably but stylishly!
Where exactly is the check-in location? +
The exact check-in address will be announced approximately one week before the event via email and our social media channels. Make sure to follow us and check your email after purchasing your ticket.
Is this event 21+ only? +
Yes, absolutely. All attendees must be 21 years of age or older. Valid photo ID will be checked at check-in. No exceptions.
What if it rains? +
The event runs rain or shine. The shuttle format keeps the party going regardless of weather. Come prepared!
Can I get a refund? +
Tickets are non-refundable but transferable. Contact us at (609) 594-2265 or PartyLikeRico@gmail.com for any ticket assistance.
How do I become a vendor or partner? +
We'd love to have local businesses involved! Visit our Vendor Inquiry page to apply, or call/text (609) 594-2265.
