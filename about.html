Who We Are

More Than an Event Company —
We Create Moments

Party Like Rico Promotions LLC was born out of a vision to elevate the Atlantic City experience beyond the casino floor. We saw an opportunity to connect locals, visitors, and businesses through the power of shared experiences — and we went all in.

Our platform was created to connect people with the best events, venues, and experiences Atlantic City and the surrounding shore towns have to offer. From shuttle party experiences and vendor pop-ups to community events and private celebrations, we coordinate it all.

We bring great energy, good people, and memorable moments together — every time.

Our Journey

The Party Like Rico Story

From a vision to elevate Atlantic City nightlife to a full-scale event promotion company — here's how we got here.

The Beginning

The Vision Takes Shape

Recognizing a gap in how Atlantic City events were promoted and experienced, the concept for Party Like Rico Promotions began to take shape — built around the idea that every experience should feel premium, personal, and memorable.

Early 2026

Party Like Rico Promotions LLC Launches

The company officially launches, with the 420 Weekend AC Bud Crawl as the debut flagship event — a shuttle party experience bringing Atlantic City's most exciting venues together under one high-energy event.

April 2026

First Major Event: The AC Bud Crawl

April 18, 2026 — the 420 Weekend AC Bud Crawl launches to a sold-out crowd, establishing Party Like Rico as a trusted name in Atlantic City event experiences.

Summer 2026 & Beyond

Expansion Across the Shore

Growing into new event formats, community partnerships, media coverage, curated travel itineraries, and a broader network of Atlantic City and Jersey Shore experiences.
